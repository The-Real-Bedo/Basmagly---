# Remaining lecture refinements

This pass strengthens the existing AI, CCNA, and IoT study notes without changing lecture/question IDs or expanding beyond the reviewed course material.

## Source coverage and additions

- **AI task environments:** AI lecture 2, PDF pp. 22–23. The summary now contrasts the lecture's taxi-driving, chess-with-a-clock, and image-analysis classifications. `AiEnvironmentLab` makes observability, number of agents, determinism, task structure, dynamics, and state/action continuity directly editable; its visibility view and one-step examples illustrate partial observation, dynamic traffic, independent image episodes, and semidynamic chess.
- **Network media and rates:** CCNA lecture 1, PDF pp. 12–14. The notes retain the source's selection criteria (distance, environment, required data rate, cost) and distinguish bandwidth from measured throughput. `NetworkMediaLab` lets learners compare copper, fiber, and wireless paths under distance, environment, and requested-rate controls.
- **IoT data flow:** IoT lecture 1, PDF pp. 7–11. A short agriculture example applies the source's three named stages—Collect, Transmit, Analyze—to the soil-moisture use case; actuation remains explicitly identified as an extension, not a fourth stage in the source figure.

## Limits

- Network-media capacities and condition factors in the simulator are declared illustrative teaching-model values, not standards, certified media limits, or measured results. Actual installations also depend on equipment, cost, and other design constraints.
- The AI scenes are simplified visual exercises, not complete task models, production classifiers, traffic predictions, or chess simulations. The chess clock advances only when the learner requests a modeled thinking step; no real-time timer runs.
- Dated IoT forecasts remain labeled as historical lecture claims rather than current measurements. No unreadable source figure or missing official worksheet has been reconstructed.
- The new component files, lecture data references, and parent-owned catalogue/renderer mappings are present. Integrated browser verification remains pending.
